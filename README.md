## Why this line

The judge weighs, in order: a horror story in a single sentence; how frightening it is; vividness; originality; sentence craft. The dread is earned, never announced — no word says "scary" or "afraid"; the facts do it. The image is concrete: a camera roll, a sleeper, a closet, a timestamp three minutes ago. The turn is the withhold: the line never says who held the phone, and the closet is inside the room, so the photographer stood over the bed moments ago. No mirror, no doll, no basement, no gore — and the last word, "ago", lands the blow: the threat is minutes old and still in the house, so the dread outlasts the line.
